White Label Lead Generation Business Model: Which of the 3 Should You Build? (2026)

A white label lead generation business model is how an agency sells lead generation under its own brand.

There are three: resell a branded platform clients run, offer a done-for-you service you run for them, or combine both as a hybrid.

The quick checklist

  • The same service can be sold three ways. Each is a different business.
  • The white-label idea in one line: one company builds it, another rebrands it.
  • Who runs the work, and how you charge, tells you which model it is.
  • Start with the model your current team can actually deliver.

  • Recurring software fees keep paying while you sleep. That is the pull of the resell model.
  • Run this check before you sell your first seat or retainer.
